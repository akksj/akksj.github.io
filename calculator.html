How this calculator works

  1. Gross salary = CTC − employer PF − gratuity provision (when those are part of your CTC). This is the salary actually paid to you.
  2. Taxable income = gross salary − ₹75,000 standard deduction.
  3. Slab tax on taxable income, FY 2026-27 new regime:
    Taxable incomeRate
    Up to ₹4,00,000Nil
    ₹4,00,001 – ₹8,00,0005%
    ₹8,00,001 – ₹12,00,00010%
    ₹12,00,001 – ₹16,00,00015%
    ₹16,00,001 – ₹20,00,00020%
    ₹20,00,001 – ₹24,00,00025%
    Above ₹24,00,00030%
  4. Rebate: if taxable income is ₹12,00,000 or less, the rebate (section 87A of the old Act, section 156 of the Income-tax Act, 2025) brings tax to zero. Just above ₹12 lakh, marginal relief caps your tax at the amount by which income exceeds ₹12 lakh, so earning ₹1 more never costs you more than ₹1 in tax.
  5. Surcharge applies only above ₹50 lakh taxable income (10%, 15% above ₹1 crore, 25% above ₹2 crore), with marginal relief.
  6. Cess: 4% health and education cess on tax plus surcharge.
  7. In-hand = gross salary − employee PF (12% of basic) − professional tax − income tax.

The "monthly in-hand (fixed)" figure leaves out variable pay, since that usually arrives once or twice a year and may not be paid in full. The variable payout is shown separately, after tax at your marginal rate.

This is an estimate, not tax advice. It assumes a resident individual on the new tax regime with only salary income, no HRA or other exemptions, and employer PF within the tax-free limits. It doesn't cover the old regime, ESOP/RSU taxation, employer NPS, reimbursements, meal cards, or mid-year joining. Your employer's TDS and payslip may differ.
