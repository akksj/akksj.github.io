FAQ

I already know system design. Is this still useful?

Probably, yes. The system design notes are for quick revision. The part most people can't find elsewhere is the India-specific offer stage: reading a CTC breakup, negotiating notice buyouts, and handling counter-offers.

Is the salary calculator accurate for my situation?

It uses FY 2026-27 new-regime tax slabs and covers standard salary components. If you're on the old regime, treat the numbers as indicative. It gives a close estimate for comparing offers, but it isn't tax advice. For anything unusual (ESOP taxation, multiple incomes), check with a CA.

Do I need Notion?

No. Everything also comes as a PDF, and the calculator is a spreadsheet that opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

How is this different from free content online?

Most free material covers DSA and system design, and it's often written for US interviews. This kit puts the Indian job-switch parts in one place: CTC vs in-hand, notice periods, buyouts, counter-offers and negotiation. It's organised so you can use it the night before an interview or a tough conversation with your manager.

Is the free calculator on this site the same as the one in the kit?

The free web calculator gives you in-hand pay for one CTC. The kit's spreadsheet adds an offer comparison sheet that puts your current pay and two offers side by side, with variable pay, joining bonus and notice-buyout cost shown separately.
