Privacy note

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This is a short, plain-English note about what this website does with your information.

This website

  • The site is a set of static pages. It doesn't set cookies and doesn't run analytics or advertising trackers.
  • The salary calculator runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you type are not sent to us or stored anywhere. They appear only in your page address so you can bookmark or share a result.
  • The site's host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and reliability. We don't use these to identify you.

The waitlist

  • If you join the waitlist, we collect your email address (and your name, if you give it).
  • We use it only to send the launch email and one reminder before the early-bird price ends. We don't sell or share it.
  • The signup form may be run by a third-party form provider, whose own privacy policy also applies.
  • You can unsubscribe at any time, or email us to have your details deleted.

Buying the kit

Payments are handled by the checkout platform (for example, Topmate). We don't see or store your card or UPI details. The platform shares your name and email with us so we can deliver the kit and handle support or refunds.
