Chapter 01 The problem I chase

Small businesses in India still run on paper.

Four real problems. Four things I built for them.

01 / 04

  1. Clinic queues

    A neighbourhood clinic's walk-in queue still runs on paper. Who is next, which slot is free and which doctor is seeing whom all live in someone's head.

    Built → MediQ Clinic: slot booking with a clash check, tokens at reception and a waiting-room board that refreshes itself.

  2. Kirana credit

    The udhaar (credit) book at a kirana counter is a paper ledger. Stock, bills and who owes what slowly drift apart.

    Built → Udhaar Khata: stock-checked bills that mix cash and credit, and per-customer balances.

  3. Timetable clashes

    A college timetable made by hand can book the same teacher or room twice, and nobody notices until the class starts.

    Built → College Timetable Generator: a class grid that refuses double bookings.

  4. CTC confusion

    Developers switching jobs get an offer as a CTC number and can't tell what will actually land in their account each month.

    Built → SwitchKit India: a free CTC to in-hand calculator (FY 2026-27, new regime), live now.

03

MERN · Kirana ledger

Udhaar Khata

A MERN ledger for a kirana counter: a product catalogue with stock and reorder levels, bills that mix cash and credit, and customer udhaar balances. Stock is checked before a bill saves, failed bills put stock back, and a collection can't exceed what the customer owes.
